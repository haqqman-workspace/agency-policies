# Subscription Renewal Policy  
**Haqqman Agency Portal**

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the standardized subscription and renewal framework for all managed services provided by Haqqman, including:

- Domain purchase & management  
- Workmail provisioning & management (e.g., Zoho Mail / SpaceMail)  
- Cloud infrastructure services  
- Website hosting & related services  
- Technical retainers  

The goal is to ensure:

- Consolidated billing  
- Predictable renewal cycles  
- Scalable automation  
- Reduced administrative overhead  
- Clear client communication  

## 2. Core Principle

Each client must have a single **Master Renewal Date (MRD)**.

All managed services under that client account will align to this date through proration.

## 3. Determining the Master Renewal Date (MRD)

### 3.1 If Haqqman Manages the Client’s Domain

The **domain renewal date** becomes the Master Renewal Date.

This applies regardless of:

- Whether the domain is registered for 1, 2, 3, or 5 years  
- Whether renewal payment has already been prepaid for multiple years  

The renewal anniversary (e.g., November 15 yearly) is used as the anchor date.

**Rationale:**  
The domain is foundational infrastructure. All other services depend on it.

### 3.2 If Haqqman Does NOT Manage the Domain

The Master Renewal Date is:

> The date of the client’s first paid managed service invoice.

This ensures every client has a clear billing anchor even if domain control is external.

## 4. Service Additions & Proration

When a client adds new services (e.g., additional mailboxes, new cloud resources, hosting upgrades):

The cost is prorated from the service start date until the next Master Renewal Date.

### Proration Formula
`Prorated Cost = (Remaining Days Until MRD ÷ 365) × Annual Service Price`


Proration applies to:

- Additional Workmail seats  
- New cloud services  
- Hosting upgrades  
- Newly added managed services  



## 5. Annual Consolidated Renewal

On the Master Renewal Date:

The client receives one consolidated renewal invoice covering:

- Total active Workmail seats  
- Active cloud services  
- Hosting  
- Domain renewal (if applicable that year)  
- Active retainers  

This ensures:

- One renewal cycle  
- Clean accounting  
- Enterprise-grade structure  



## 6. Domain-Specific Notes

### 6.1 Multi-Year Domain Registrations

If a domain is registered for multiple years:

- The renewal anniversary still exists annually.  
- The domain charge will only appear in renewal invoices in the year it is due.  
- Other services will renew annually regardless.  

### 6.2 Client-Managed Domains

If a client manages their own domain:

- Haqqman is not responsible for domain expiration.  
- Service disruptions caused by domain expiration remain the client’s responsibility.  
- MRD remains based on first paid service.  



## 7. Seat-Based Services (Workmail)

For Workmail services:

- Seats are billed annually.  
- Seat additions are prorated to MRD.  
- Seat removals do not generate refunds mid-cycle unless otherwise approved.  

At renewal:

`Total Active Seats × Annual Seat Price`




## 8. Non-Payment Policy

If a renewal invoice remains unpaid past the grace period:

- Workmail accounts may be suspended  
- Cloud services may be paused  
- Domain auto-renew may be disabled  
- Retainer services may be halted  

Restoration may incur administrative fees.



## 9. Exceptional Cases

Exceptions to this policy may apply for:

- Enterprise contracts  
- Custom multi-year agreements  
- Strategic partnerships  

Any deviation must be documented in writing within the client’s account file.



## 10. Policy Governance

This policy applies to all new and migrated clients moving forward.

Legacy billing structures may be aligned to this model gradually.
